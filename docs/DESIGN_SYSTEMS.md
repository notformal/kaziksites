# Three design directions

## Aurora Play — kinetic future

Near-black space, acid green and cyan, oversized Manrope headlines, glowing spherical hero artifact, sharp conversion CTAs. Built for a fast, modern, game-first impression.

## Ember Club — nightlife energy

Black-cherry surfaces, amber and hot pink, italic uppercase hero language, reward-led messaging. Built to feel social, urgent, and event-driven without fake scarcity.

## Royale House — quiet luxury

Deep green-black, restrained gold and mint, editorial Playfair display type, curated language and gentler contrast. Built for trust, taste, and a premium audience.

All three share an 8px spacing rhythm, large touch targets, a conversion path from hero to catalog to playable demo, clear demo-only disclosure, and one maintainable component system.
